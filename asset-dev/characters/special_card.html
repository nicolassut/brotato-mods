What is and isn't decided here

Decided: reveal happens in the shop and previews the next wave, wave 1 rolls nothing, every modifier is temporary, the pool has more bad than good, good ones get slightly rarer as the run climbs, and endless repeats the pool.

Not decided: the name, and the character's own baseline kit. The card above deliberately shows no stat block, because inventing one would be me making that call for you. Between rolls he currently has no identity at all, which is the main thing still to solve.

Colour on the briefing is doing real work: green good, red bad, purple mixed, matching Brotato's own color_positive, color_negative and CURSE_COLOR. Note "Blood Money" is flagged (shop only) because it is one of the eight next-shop-scoped modifiers rather than wave-scoped.
